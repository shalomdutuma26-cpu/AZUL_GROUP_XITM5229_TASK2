1. IT Department Staff Structure & Hierarchies

The African Foods Company (Ltd) IT infrastructure operates under a centralized management model to maintain system continuity across the Cape Town logistics hub and regional nodes.

  • Chief Information Officer (CIO): Directs global technological strategies and reports directly to corporate executives.
  • IT Security Manager: Oversees data center network integrity and manages corporate VLAN firewalls.
  • Systems Administrator: Manages the central Sage ERP environment, database records, and server backups.
  • Service Desk Supervisor: Controls tier-1 support desks and coordinates technical assistance across branches.
  • Technical Support Technicians: Provide direct device maintenance and network provisioning for warehouse floor managers.

2. IT Security Policy Guidelines (VLAN 20)

Strict access security parameters designed to prevent unauthorized external intrusion and insulate operational data spaces.

  • All network terminal entries must utilize active Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) validation.
  • Core financial databases and logistics servers must remain isolated inside designated secure VLAN 20 zones.
  • Administrative system privileges are allocated strictly following a least-privilege framework.
  • Security definitions and system patch matrices are automatically updated weekly at 02:00 SAST.
  • Any detected system connection anomaly triggers an automated isolation response from the central firewall.

3. Acceptable Use Policy (VLAN 10)

Regulatory rules defining clear user parameters for all employees accessing company-owned equipment and connection layers.

  • Company hardware assets are provided strictly for official food production and logistics operations.
  • The installation of unauthorized third-party software applications on company endpoints is completely prohibited.
  • Employees must not bypass corporate network filters or use personal storage media without technical validation.
  • Corporate email infrastructure must never be used for external marketing initiatives or personal messaging.
  • Network traffic logs across the user VLAN 10 zone are monitored continuously to maintain bandwidth performance.

4. Acquisition Procedures & Procurement Approvals

Standardized workflows governing hardware updates, asset provisioning, and vendor allocation steps.

  • All technical hardware requisitions must originate through a formal digital ticket request form.
  • Procurement requests exceeding R50,000 require a multi-vendor evaluation matrix with three distinct quotes.
  • Final financial approvals must be authorized by both the IT Department Manager and the Logistics Director.
  • Acquired assets are recorded in the central inventory log with unique barcode parameters before deployment.
  • Legacy equipment models are decommissioned following strict electronic waste disposal protocols.

5. Service Desk & Technical Help Desk Procedures

Incident logging rules, processing windows, and escalation paths designed to reduce platform connection drops.

  • All technical incidents must be formally logged via the internal tracking portal or support line.
  • Tickets are immediately categorized by impact: Critical (ERP offline), Medium (Printer fault), or Low (Inquiry).
  • Critical core system failures require a technician response window within 15 minutes of initial logging.
  • Unresolved category-1 tickets automatically escalate to the Senior Systems Engineer after 60 minutes.
  • Tickets are formally closed only after receiving explicit verification and clearance from the logging user.
